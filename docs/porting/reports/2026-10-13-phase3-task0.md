## Scope

1. Bootstrap `src/test`.
2. Add a Gradle switch for Metamorph, MCLib and Baubles on the dev runtime.

## Assumptions
- Server-side results stand in for single-player world entry for load, save and NBT. Rendering, GUI, client packets and the client half of integrations are not covered.
- Hand-made `/summon` NBT is not the normal player path (Ship Spawn Egg), so the round trip shows NBT persistence, not egg spawning.

## Unknowns
- **Client world entry is still unverified.** This covers:
  - rendering
  - GUIs
  - Ship Spawn Egg use
  - feeding
  - the client half of MOD-01 (an `S2CSpawnParticle` attack animation with Metamorph absent)

  Each needs a player in the world.
- **Entity ticking without a player.** Vanilla `WorldServer.updateEntities` stops entity updates about 300 ticks after the last player leaves, and the server never had a player. Entities ticked only during the window after each start; a vanilla zombie control froze the same way. AI, movement and combat were therefore barely exercised.
- **Unexplained:** after the restart, 3 of 5 summoned ships disappeared within about 35 s (the 3 with the lowest max health: Akatsuki, Destroyer I, Hibiki). There was no log line and no Ship Spawn Egg dropped; a Grudge item was found nearby. Possible causes:
  - death from fall damage (they spawned with Health 4.0 and fell about 12 blocks) before `CanDrop` was set (`BasicEntityShip.java:1866`, after 10 ticks)
  - a despawn rule
  - something else

  This is related to ENT-02 and is not diagnosed.
- Summoned ships spawned with Health 4.0, below their max health. That is plausible for the `/summon` path, which skips egg initialization. Not investigated.

## Risks
- INV-12 makes `/summon` useless for owned ships, so test setups and admins cannot spawn them by command. The fix sits next to #7 and must keep key names (ADR-0002).
- `getBoolean` on absent keys (for example `CanDrop`) returns false, not the default. Under ADR-0002 ("absent keys mean keep defaults") the #7 fix should cover absent keys, not only absent sections.
